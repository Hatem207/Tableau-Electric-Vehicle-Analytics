# Tableau-Electric-Vehicle-Analytics

## Project Overview

![Dashboard](Images/Overview.png)

This Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of electric vehicle registrations and adoption trends. It helps users explore EV growth, vehicle types, manufacturers, models, electric range, CAFV eligibility, and geographic distribution through interactive dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The dashboard is designed to provide a clear overview of the electric vehicle market and support data-driven insights into EV adoption patterns.

## Business Problem

The electric vehicle market is growing rapidly, creating a need to understand adoption trends, vehicle manufacturers, popular models, geographic distribution, and vehicle characteristics.

This dashboard analyzes electric vehicle population data to answer important business questions such as:

* How many electric vehicles are registered?
* What percentage of vehicles are BEVs and PHEVs?
* Which manufacturers dominate the EV market?
* Which EV models are the most popular?
* How has EV adoption changed over the years?
* Which states have the highest concentration of EVs?
* What percentage of vehicles are eligible for Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle programs?
* What is the average electric driving range?

## Key Insights

* The dashboard contains approximately **149,804 registered electric vehicles** in the analyzed dataset.
* Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) represent the majority of the analyzed EV population.
* Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs) represent a smaller portion of the market.
* Tesla is the leading EV manufacturer in the dataset by a significant margin.
* Model Y and Model 3 are among the most popular EV models.
* EV registrations show strong growth in more recent model years.
* A significant portion of vehicles has an unknown CAFV eligibility status.
* Vehicles are geographically concentrated in specific U.S. states.
* Electric vehicle adoption has increased substantially in recent model years.

## Calculated Fields

Several calculated fields were created in Tableau to support the analysis.

### BEV Vehicles

Identifies Battery Electric Vehicles.

### BEV %

Calculates the percentage of Battery Electric Vehicles within the selected dataset.

### PHEV Vehicles

Identifies Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles.

### PHEV %

Calculates the percentage of Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles within the selected dataset.

These calculated fields allow the dashboard KPIs to update dynamically when users interact with the filters.
